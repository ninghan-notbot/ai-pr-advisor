# Knowledge scope

The package supplies 40 original decision cards in eight groups: service recovery, digital/AI,
products and health, people and community, claims, media, markets and operational governance.
Cards draw on linked public guidance and project reasoning; each states questions, actions,
coordination, communication, failure conditions and scope. They are not a collection of verified
case-study outcomes and do not establish that a particular intervention will work.

The Skill reads only its bundled resources and user-authorized tools. It has no connection to a
private case store. Check the source's jurisdiction and date before applying specific legal or
pricing statements. See the [source register](../references/sources.md).

## v0.5 expansion (2026-11-17)

The private registry now contains 1,258 conservatively deduplicated research event clusters:
1,150 CANDIDATE, 108 INITIAL_REVIEW, 0 independently VERIFIED. The original 301 records and all
v0.4 evidence remain preserved. 1,142 additional source intakes (including two individually reviewed
Australian events), less 185 duplicate/conservatively grouped entries, give 957 net additional events.

New intake uses full CPSC recall records, FDA enforcement event groups, GOV.UK CMA case bodies
and Chinese official enforcement case sections, not headlines. FDA product rows sharing an event
identifier count once; repeat notices, joint investigations and later updates are not extra cases.
A conservative same-firm/reason/date grouping also avoids multiplying related recall entries.
Regulatory events are not automatically PR crises or evidence of effective communications.

Coverage includes China, the US and other markets, with consumer products/recalls overrepresented.
Candidate records preserve dates, source captures and hashes, available response/outcome fields,
provisional tags and explicit missing media/follow-up review. Unknown is not zero, and a closed
regulatory case is not proof that customers received redress. Independent semantic duplicate review,
media coverage and long-term outcomes remain incomplete across the bulk collection.

Only six scoped, newly initially reviewed public precedent summaries enter the development Skill.
They connect to existing cards; no raw private registry, original article corpus or private material
is bundled. These summaries distinguish observations from project inferences and failure conditions.
The installed Skill has no ability to query the private library. V2 controlled retrieval remains deferred.

Two confidential legacy records remain protected and are excluded from the public-source target count.
The public-source-eligible count is **1,256**, not 1,258. This eligibility is not publication approval;
only the six selected new precedent summaries are approved for the candidate package.
