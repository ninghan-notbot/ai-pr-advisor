# Knowledge scope

The package supplies 40 original decision cards in eight groups: service recovery, digital/AI,
products and health, people and community, claims, media, markets and operational governance.
Cards draw on linked public guidance and project reasoning; each states questions, actions,
coordination, communication, failure conditions and scope. They are not a collection of verified
case-study outcomes and do not establish that a particular intervention will work.

The Skill reads only its bundled resources and user-authorized tools. It has no connection to a
private case store. Check the source's jurisdiction and date before applying specific legal or
pricing statements. See the [source register](../references/sources.md).
