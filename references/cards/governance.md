# Governance / 按需决策卡

Adapt these suggestions to the user’s facts, resources and authority. A proposed action remains a proposal until approved.

<a id="k36"></a>

## K36 优先决策 / Decision triage

- **Use for:** A vague complaint could be a routine failure or an urgent hazard.
- **Decision questions:** What harm is continuing, who is affected now and what decision cannot wait?
- **Practical response:** Give a useful immediate step, ask the missing fact that changes the route and compare proportionate options as evidence develops.
- **Organization:** The person with authority leads; involve specialists only for the decisions that require them.
- **Communication / media:** Lead with what people can do and the next update; avoid reflexive silence until every detail is known.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** No formal report does not mean no usable internal information; an internal view also does not justify universal assurance.
- **Basis:** [CDC CERC](https://www.cdc.gov/cerc/php/about/index.html). Public health emergency communication; adapted operational advice is the project's own synthesis.

<a id="k37"></a>

## K37 审批缺口 / Approval gap

- **Use for:** A proposed remedy exceeds budget or approval scope.
- **Decision questions:** What is already authorized, what extra resource is needed and who may approve it by when?
- **Practical response:** Separate lawful or contractual obligations from discretionary proposals. Request a specific resource and offer a feasible interim route while approval is pending.
- **Organization:** The decision owner requests approval; the authorized budget holder decides; frontline staff receive exact approved limits.
- **Communication / media:** Draft proposed offers conditionally until approved; communicate actual available assistance without disguising limits.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** Never convert a recommendation into approved compensation, extended warranty or a promise of new spending.
- **Basis:** [PRSA ethics](https://www.prsa.org/professional-development/prsa-resources/ethics). Honesty, independence and fairness; professional ethical guidance is not law.

<a id="k38"></a>

## K38 精简执行 / Lean execution

- **Use for:** The same plan must work for a small firm and a distributed organization.
- **Decision questions:** How many people, hours and funds are available; which checks are essential?
- **Practical response:** For a small team combine roles with a consequential-promise check. For a network delegate within limits and resolve escalation bottlenecks.
- **Organization:** Name the decision maker, doer and checker; do not invent departments or assume one person can do simultaneous tasks.
- **Communication / media:** Match response channels to staffing and the actual audience, with a realistic update cadence.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** More committees, tables or steps do not prove quality; cut work that does not change a decision.
- **Basis:** [CDC plain language](https://www.cdc.gov/health-literacy/php/develop-materials/plain-language.html). Audience-oriented wording, prioritized messages and usable instructions.

<a id="k39"></a>

## K39 结果评估 / Outcome measurement

- **Use for:** A communications effort reports reach but business harm continues.
- **Decision questions:** What user behavior or service result should improve, compared with what baseline?
- **Practical response:** Separate messages sent, understanding, remedy uptake and actual service outcomes. Track cost and plausible alternative causes.
- **Organization:** Operations owns service data; communications checks understanding; finance checks costs; leadership reviews the trade-off.
- **Communication / media:** Report denominators, time windows and limits; do not call impressions restored trust.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** Correlation after a campaign does not prove causality; a small survey may not represent all affected people.
- **Basis:** [EPA public participation](https://www.epa.gov/international-cooperation/public-participation-guide). Meaningful public involvement in environmental decisions; adaptation to other organizations is an inference.

## K40 复盘与复发 / Learning without guarantees

- **Use for:** A team wants to announce a permanent fix after an incident.
- **Decision questions:** Which process failed, what has changed and what evidence would show the change is working?
- **Practical response:** Assign corrective actions and due dates, check the end-to-end process and define a recurrence trigger for escalation.
- **Organization:** The operational owner closes actions on evidence; frontline staff report recurrence; leadership reviews overdue items.
- **Communication / media:** Explain completed fixes, remaining work and the next check instead of saying the problem can never happen again.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** A new policy document or training attendance alone does not demonstrate changed outcomes.
- **Basis:** [OSHA worker participation](https://www.osha.gov/safety-management/worker-participation). Worker and contractor participation without retaliation. Employment-law application remains jurisdiction-specific.
