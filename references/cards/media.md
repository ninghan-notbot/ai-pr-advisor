# Media / 按需决策卡

Adapt these suggestions to the user’s facts, resources and authority. A proposed action remains a proposal until approved.

<a id="k26"></a>

## K26 媒体匹配 / Editorial fit

- **Use for:** The organization needs to decide whether and where to pitch.
- **Decision questions:** Who needs this information, why would an independent newsroom cover it, and what evidence is ready?
- **Practical response:** Compare direct audience channels, local or specialist coverage and a broader pitch; choose a public-interest angle supported by facts.
- **Organization:** Communications checks current desk fit; an accountable expert answers substantive questions; leadership approves attributable statements.
- **Communication / media:** Use official public contact routes and verify a recent relevant story before claiming a desk's current interests.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** A news-tip route is not a guaranteed promotional service; no mass contact or coverage promise.
- **Basis:** [AP news tips](https://www.ap.org/contact-us/news-tips/). Official public-interest tip route; not a promotional pitch promise.

<a id="k27"></a>

## K27 采访准备 / Spokesperson interview

- **Use for:** An interview is scheduled while facts are still developing.
- **Decision questions:** What can the spokesperson substantiate, what remains unknown and who can update the answer?
- **Practical response:** Prepare a few supported points, difficult questions and a route for correcting errors after the interview.
- **Organization:** Subject owner validates facts; communications rehearses concise answers; authorized spokesperson owns public commitments.
- **Communication / media:** Answer the question directly, acknowledge uncertainty and offer a dated follow-up rather than inventing certainty.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** An agreed briefing does not confer editorial control; do not conceal a material hazard behind a bridge phrase.
- **Basis:** [CDC CERC](https://www.cdc.gov/cerc/php/about/index.html). Public health emergency communication; adapted operational advice is the project's own synthesis.

<a id="k28"></a>

## K28 付费内容 / Sponsored content

- **Use for:** A commercial placement is being considered.
- **Decision questions:** Is sponsorship acceptable for the objective, what deliverables are quoted and who controls labeling?
- **Practical response:** Compare the paid route with owned communication and editorial outreach; approve only a scoped deliverable and actual budget.
- **Organization:** Marketing or communications verifies vendor scope; finance approves spend; legal reviews disclosure obligations when needed.
- **Communication / media:** Clearly label the relationship and retain the distinction from independent editorial judgment.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** Do not buy disguised coverage, invent audience numbers or treat a sales contact as a newsroom promise.
- **Basis:** [FTC influencer disclosures](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers). Material connections and clear disclosure; current local rules must be checked.

<a id="k29"></a>

## K29 新闻稿分发 / Release distribution

- **Use for:** A team equates distribution with independent media attention.
- **Decision questions:** What audience needs a release, which circuit and word count are required, and what is the approved spending purpose?
- **Practical response:** Check the current public rate and extras, then compare distribution with a direct stakeholder update.
- **Organization:** The release owner verifies claims; finance approves the complete quote; an authorized person releases it.
- **Communication / media:** A dated starting price is a planning reference only. Count distribution and earned reporting separately.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** Vendor editorial review is not independent journalism; the lowest listed price is not the final cross-border quote.
- **Basis:** [Business Wire pricing](https://www.businesswire.com/pricing). Dated distribution starting price; neither a firm quote nor independent editorial coverage.

<a id="k30"></a>

## K30 公开更正 / Public correction

- **Use for:** A material statement was wrong or incomplete.
- **Decision questions:** Where did it spread, who may act on it and what corrected fact is established?
- **Practical response:** Correct the original location and relevant redistributions, preserve a dated correction and directly alert those facing material consequences.
- **Organization:** Fact owner verifies the correction; communications coordinates channels; service addresses resulting harm.
- **Communication / media:** Say what changed without quietly rewriting a consequential error; offer a reliable current version.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** Do not repeat a harmful falsehood unnecessarily or imply all recipients have seen the correction.
- **Basis:** [PRSA ethics](https://www.prsa.org/professional-development/prsa-resources/ethics). Honesty, independence and fairness; professional ethical guidance is not law.
