# Digital / 按需决策卡

Adapt these suggestions to the user’s facts, resources and authority. Use the working basis in [SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md#one-basis-for-action-and-copy).

<a id="k06"></a>

## K06 数据暴露 / Data exposure

- **Use for:** Information may have reached unintended recipients.
- **Decision questions:** What data, permissions, logs and affected users are known; is exposure continuing?
- **Practical response:** Contain the affected route proportionately and preserve evidence. Separate exposure, access and misuse; arrange a scoped investigation.
- **Organization:** Security or the technical owner leads containment; privacy counsel checks notification duties; support provides safe assistance.
- **Communication / media:** Give verified protective actions and update times without publishing exploitable details or victim data.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** Do not say no breach from a small integrity sample or erase evidence while fixing the leak.
- **Basis:** [FTC data breach response](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/data-breach-response-guide-business). Response coordination, evidence preservation and scoped notification. US guidance; local legal duties need current review.

<a id="k07"></a>

## K07 自动决策申诉 / Automated decisions

- **Use for:** An algorithm denies services or allocates benefits unexpectedly.
- **Decision questions:** What decision is automated, who can override it, and which people cannot appeal?
- **Practical response:** Provide a human review route and pause the harmful decision path where justified; test inputs and affected subgroups.
- **Organization:** The service owner retains decision authority; technical staff investigate; frontline staff communicate review access.
- **Communication / media:** Explain what the system did and the human correction route in ordinary language.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** Accuracy on average does not demonstrate fairness for every subgroup; avoid claiming a NIST certification.
- **Basis:** [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework). AI risk management; framework revision is in progress, so no certification or complete adoption is claimed.

<a id="k08"></a>

## K08 聊天机器人错误 / Chatbot errors

- **Use for:** An assistant gives incorrect service information or creates unauthorized promises.
- **Decision questions:** Which answer was displayed, what source produced it, and did people rely on it?
- **Practical response:** Correct the knowledge source and affected answers, notify materially affected users and route unresolved cases to a person.
- **Organization:** Product owns the fix; support verifies remedy needs; the business owner determines commitments and funding.
- **Communication / media:** Acknowledge the organization owns its service channel; say which corrections have actually been deployed.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** Do not blame the model to evade responsibility or promise error-free future output.
- **Basis:** [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework). AI risk management; framework revision is in progress, so no certification or complete adoption is claimed.

<a id="k09"></a>

## K09 合成内容与冒充 / Synthetic impersonation

- **Use for:** A fabricated message or clip appears to represent the organization.
- **Decision questions:** Can provenance be checked, what actual harm is occurring and where are people encountering it?
- **Practical response:** Preserve the item and verify authorized channels. Correct the consequential false claim and offer a trusted verification route.
- **Organization:** A designated incident owner coordinates security, legal and communications; obtain approval before paid amplification.
- **Communication / media:** Use a narrowly targeted correction with evidence; avoid unnecessarily republishing harmful content.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** Unfamiliar content is not automatically fake; do not invent a forensic finding.
- **Basis:** [PRSA ethics](https://www.prsa.org/professional-development/prsa-resources/ethics). Honesty, independence and fairness; professional ethical guidance is not law.

<a id="k10"></a>

## K10 服务宕机 / Service outage

- **Use for:** A digital service is unavailable and recovery time is uncertain.
- **Decision questions:** Which functions fail, which dependencies matter, and is there a reliable workaround?
- **Practical response:** Restore the critical journey first; label recovery estimates as estimates and set the next update even if no fix is ready.
- **Organization:** Technical incident lead owns recovery; operations validates the workaround; support records user impact.
- **Communication / media:** Use a dated status notice accessible outside the failed service; communicate changes in scope.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** An internal recovery target is not a customer guarantee. Do not shut unaffected functions without a reason.
- **Basis:** [FTC data breach response](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/data-breach-response-guide-business). Response coordination, evidence preservation and scoped notification. US guidance; local legal duties need current review.
