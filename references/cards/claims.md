# Claims / 按需决策卡

Adapt these suggestions to the user’s facts, resources and authority. A proposed action remains a proposal until approved.

<a id="k21"></a>

## K21 产品发布边界 / Product launch claims

- **Use for:** A launch promises capabilities or delivery dates beyond what is ready.
- **Decision questions:** What can be demonstrated now, what is a plan and what evidence supports availability?
- **Practical response:** Separate current capability, limited pilot and roadmap. Align order-taking with actual delivery capacity.
- **Organization:** Product verifies readiness; operations confirms fulfillment; marketing and authorized leadership approve commitments.
- **Communication / media:** Use a simple substantiated proposition and disclose material demo conditions; prepare support for limitations.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** Attention and preorders are not proof of sustained product success; avoid unqualified superiority claims.
- **Basis:** [PRSA ethics](https://www.prsa.org/professional-development/prsa-resources/ethics). Honesty, independence and fairness; professional ethical guidance is not law.

<a id="k22"></a>

## K22 环保主张 / Environmental claims

- **Use for:** A broad green claim may exceed the evidence.
- **Decision questions:** Which lifecycle stage, comparison, metric and period does the evidence actually cover?
- **Practical response:** Narrow the claim to the supported benefit, disclose material trade-offs and fix the unsupported wording across channels.
- **Organization:** Sustainability or technical owner validates evidence; marketing revises assets; legal reviews local requirements.
- **Communication / media:** Make qualifications prominent and understandable rather than hiding them in a footnote.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** One recycled component does not establish the whole product is environmentally beneficial.
- **Basis:** [FTC Green Guides summary](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/environmental-claims-summary-green-guides). Specific substantiated environmental claims, not broad unqualified benefits. The historical guidance is not a claim about every jurisdiction.

<a id="k23"></a>

## K23 达人与赞助披露 / Influencer disclosure

- **Use for:** Endorsements involve gifts, payment or another material connection.
- **Decision questions:** What benefit was provided, did the endorser actually use the product, and can viewers see the disclosure?
- **Practical response:** Require truthful experience-based claims and conspicuous relationship disclosure in the relevant language and format.
- **Organization:** Marketing owns contracts and review; the endorser remains responsible for their statements; compliance reviews sensitive claims.
- **Communication / media:** Paid content must be labeled where people encounter it; do not present it as independent coverage.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** A platform label or a buried hashtag may not suffice; no fabricated testimonials.
- **Basis:** [FTC influencer disclosures](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers). Material connections and clear disclosure; current local rules must be checked.

<a id="k24"></a>

## K24 评论失真 / Review integrity

- **Use for:** A team wants to hide criticism or inflate customer reviews.
- **Decision questions:** Which reviews are authentic, which violate rules and how does the platform handle disputes?
- **Practical response:** Resolve real complaints and use legitimate moderation channels for abuse; separate feedback from incentives and do not condition remedies on silence.
- **Organization:** Service repairs the issue; platform liaison handles rule-based reports; management stops manipulated review practices.
- **Communication / media:** Respond with specific assistance and protect personal details; explain corrections transparently.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** Negative feedback is not automatically misinformation; do not manufacture consensus or fake customer experience.
- **Basis:** [China consumer protection implementing regulation](https://app.www.gov.cn/govdata/gov/202403/19/513111/article.html). Official 2024 regulation; scope-specific current legal review is required. No universal refund deadline is inferred.

<a id="k25"></a>

## K25 价格与自动续费 / Pricing clarity

- **Use for:** Customers are surprised by checkout charges or renewals.
- **Decision questions:** What price was shown, what consent was obtained and who can reverse a disputed charge?
- **Practical response:** Reconcile the purchase journey, correct material price disclosures and give a practical cancellation or dispute route.
- **Organization:** Commerce and finance fix charges; product checks interface behavior; an authorized owner approves discretionary relief.
- **Communication / media:** Explain the total charge and billing period plainly; distinguish lawful obligations from proposed goodwill.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** A new notice does not retrospectively establish consent. Check the applicable consumer rules.
- **Basis:** [China consumer protection implementing regulation](https://app.www.gov.cn/govdata/gov/202403/19/513111/article.html). Official 2024 regulation; scope-specific current legal review is required. No universal refund deadline is inferred.
