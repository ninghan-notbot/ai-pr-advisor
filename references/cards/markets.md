# Markets / 按需决策卡

Adapt these suggestions to the user’s facts, resources and authority. A proposed action remains a proposal until approved.

<a id="k31"></a>

## K31 双语确定性 / Bilingual certainty

- **Use for:** Chinese and English drafts describe the same evolving incident.
- **Decision questions:** Do cause, scope, quantities, dates, action status and certainty match sentence by sentence?
- **Practical response:** Maintain a shared fact sheet, then check each market draft against it. Adapt access details and examples without changing the facts.
- **Organization:** One fact owner approves the shared ledger; local reviewers check meaning and practical service routes.
- **Communication / media:** Keep suspected versus confirmed and planned versus completed equally clear in both languages.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** Translation fluency does not establish factual consistency; avoid strengthening causal claims in one language.
- **Basis:** [CDC plain language](https://www.cdc.gov/health-literacy/php/develop-materials/plain-language.html). Audience-oriented wording, prioritized messages and usable instructions.

<a id="k32"></a>

## K32 总部与属地授权 / Headquarters and local authority

- **Use for:** A local team cannot act while headquarters controls approval.
- **Decision questions:** Which decisions need global consistency and which can be delegated within a defined limit?
- **Practical response:** Set an explicit local authority envelope, escalation deadline and fallback if a decision maker is unavailable.
- **Organization:** Headquarters owns shared facts and major commitments; local operations owns accessible execution; finance confirms spending authority.
- **Communication / media:** Use one factual foundation with locally useful service instructions and named approval responsibility.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** A global statement is not a local remedy; do not let absent approval become an invented authorization.
- **Basis:** [EPA public participation](https://www.epa.gov/international-cooperation/public-participation-guide). Meaningful public involvement in environmental decisions; adaptation to other organizations is an inference.

<a id="k33"></a>

## K33 跨时区更新 / Cross-time-zone updates

- **Use for:** Customers in different markets receive inconsistent updates.
- **Decision questions:** Which time zones, deadlines and channels matter to affected users?
- **Practical response:** Use explicit date and time zone, synchronized version numbers and a handover record; agree who owns the next update.
- **Organization:** Regional support hands unresolved cases to a named owner; operations validates service hours and language coverage.
- **Communication / media:** Publish the next update time in usable local form and maintain a common reference version.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** Do not promise round-the-clock support without staffing or treat a local date as globally unambiguous.
- **Basis:** [CDC plain language](https://www.cdc.gov/health-literacy/php/develop-materials/plain-language.html). Audience-oriented wording, prioritized messages and usable instructions.

<a id="k34"></a>

## K34 弱势受众渠道 / Reaching underserved audiences

- **Use for:** A digital notice misses people with language, literacy or access barriers.
- **Decision questions:** Who is missing the message, how can they act and who can help without taking over their choices?
- **Practical response:** Test a short instruction through accessible channels and trusted service points; verify understanding rather than reach alone.
- **Organization:** Service lead and local partners coordinate practical assistance; safeguarding or privacy review protects individuals.
- **Communication / media:** Provide an offline or assisted route where needed and avoid stereotypes about an entire population.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** Translated text alone may not be usable; do not publish identifiable beneficiary stories without appropriate permission.
- **Basis:** [DOJ effective communication](https://www.ada.gov/resources/effective-communication/). US accessibility guidance. Check applicable law and individual needs; do not assume one format serves everyone.

<a id="k35"></a>

## K35 政策与公共咨询 / Public consultation

- **Use for:** An institution seeks feedback on a proposed change.
- **Decision questions:** What is open to influence, who is affected and how will input affect the decision?
- **Practical response:** State the decision scope, collect meaningful alternatives and report how input was considered, including unresolved disagreements.
- **Organization:** The accountable policy owner links consultation to decisions; facilitators ensure access and record responses.
- **Communication / media:** Explain trade-offs and deadlines accurately; distinguish information provision from consultation.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** Do not manufacture public support or claim consensus from a self-selected sample.
- **Basis:** [EPA public participation](https://www.epa.gov/international-cooperation/public-participation-guide). Meaningful public involvement in environmental decisions; adaptation to other organizations is an inference.
