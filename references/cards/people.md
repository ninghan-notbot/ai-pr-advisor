# People / 按需决策卡

Adapt these suggestions to the user’s facts, resources and authority. Use the working basis in [SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md#one-basis-for-action-and-copy).

<a id="k16"></a>

## K16 员工提出问题 / Employee concerns

- **Use for:** Employees raise operational or safety concerns publicly or internally.
- **Decision questions:** Is harm ongoing, how can workers report without retaliation, and who has authority to investigate?
- **Practical response:** Protect reporting channels, preserve relevant evidence and assign an accountable response with a feedback date.
- **Organization:** The responsible manager involves worker representatives and qualified specialists where needed; HR does not replace operational ownership.
- **Communication / media:** Inform employees of actions before asking them to repeat a public message; avoid coercing silence.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** Do not identify complainants unnecessarily or confuse a completed interview with resolved conditions.
- **Basis:** [OSHA worker participation](https://www.osha.gov/safety-management/worker-participation). Worker and contractor participation without retaliation. Employment-law application remains jurisdiction-specific.

<a id="k17"></a>

## K17 承包商协调 / Contractor coordination

- **Use for:** A failure crosses employer, supplier or contractor boundaries.
- **Decision questions:** Who controls the work, which workers face the risk and what information is not crossing the boundary?
- **Practical response:** Agree immediate work controls and a shared handover, while preserving each party's responsibilities.
- **Organization:** A lead with decision authority coordinates host managers and contractor supervisors; include temporary workers in instructions.
- **Communication / media:** Use consistent operational instructions; disclose material uncertainty rather than hiding behind contractual blame.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** A contract allocating liability does not itself fix the dangerous or failed process.
- **Basis:** [OSHA worker participation](https://www.osha.gov/safety-management/worker-participation). Worker and contractor participation without retaliation. Employment-law application remains jurisdiction-specific.

## K18 社区施工争议 / Community disruption

- **Use for:** A site change or construction project disrupts nearby people.
- **Decision questions:** Who is affected at what times, what choices remain open, and how will feedback change the plan?
- **Practical response:** Offer feasible mitigation options and a response process linking concerns to decisions; document which requests can or cannot be met.
- **Organization:** Project owner authorizes changes; site operations checks feasibility; community liaison reports back.
- **Communication / media:** Use accessible notices, meetings or direct outreach matched to residents; publish the response to concerns.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** Consultation after all decisions are fixed must not be presented as shared decision making.
- **Basis:** [EPA public participation](https://www.epa.gov/international-cooperation/public-participation-guide). Meaningful public involvement in environmental decisions; adaptation to other organizations is an inference.

## K19 无障碍沟通 / Accessible service

- **Use for:** People cannot use notices, bookings or remedies in the offered format.
- **Decision questions:** What individual communication need is unmet, and what equally useful alternative can be provided?
- **Practical response:** Ask about the needed format, provide an effective channel and test the complete interaction with the user where possible.
- **Organization:** Service owner coordinates accessible formats and staff support; an authorized manager resolves resource requests promptly.
- **Communication / media:** Provide key actions in more than one usable form; do not force a smartphone-only remedy.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** Disability is not a single audience category; legal duties and reasonable options depend on context.
- **Basis:** [DOJ effective communication](https://www.ada.gov/resources/effective-communication/). US accessibility guidance. Check applicable law and individual needs; do not assume one format serves everyone.

## K20 公益资金信任 / Nonprofit fund concerns

- **Use for:** Donors or beneficiaries question how promised resources were used.
- **Decision questions:** What was restricted, what records exist and who can authorize a correction?
- **Practical response:** Reconcile commitments with actual spending and service delivery; protect beneficiary privacy while explaining material discrepancies.
- **Organization:** The accountable officer and finance lead review records; governance body approves any use changes or independent review.
- **Communication / media:** Report defined facts, unresolved items and the next accounting milestone; show effects on beneficiaries.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** Do not use a positive beneficiary story to distract from missing accounts or reallocate restricted funds without authority.
- **Basis:** [PRSA ethics](https://www.prsa.org/professional-development/prsa-resources/ethics). Honesty, independence and fairness; professional ethical guidance is not law.
