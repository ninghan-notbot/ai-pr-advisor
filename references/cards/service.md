# Service / 按需决策卡

Adapt these suggestions to the user’s facts, resources and authority. A proposed action remains a proposal until approved.

<a id="k01"></a>

## K01 逾期订单 / Late orders

- **Use for:** Repeated late deliveries or collections erode trust.
- **Decision questions:** Which customers are still waiting, which dates were promised, and what can actually be delivered today?
- **Practical response:** Separate ready stock from uncertain arrivals. Contact affected customers with a supported date or a dated next update and a cancellation route.
- **Organization:** Operations owns the queue; customer support records choices; the owner authorizes any added delivery cost.
- **Communication / media:** Use direct service notices before broad publicity; protect order details in public replies.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** Do not treat a supplier estimate as a confirmed customer promise; US merchandise rules do not apply unchanged to every service.
- **Basis:** [FTC merchandise orders](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/business-guide-ftcs-mail-internet-or-telephone-order-merchandise-rule). Shipment representations, delay consent and refunds for covered US merchandise. Do not generalize fixed deadlines to services or other markets.

<a id="k02"></a>

## K02 退款兑现 / Refund execution

- **Use for:** A refund announcement is not reaching customers.
- **Decision questions:** Is the right to refund established, who can release money, and what portion is goodwill rather than returning principal?
- **Practical response:** Track requested, approved, submitted and received refunds separately. Give a reference and an escalation route.
- **Organization:** Finance releases funds; service staff verify eligibility without repeatedly demanding the same documents; the owner approves discretionary extras.
- **Communication / media:** Describe each person's actual refund stage, not a blanket claim that everyone has been paid.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** A proposed voucher cannot silently replace a refund. Check jurisdiction and contractual rights.
- **Basis:** [China consumer protection implementing regulation](https://app.www.gov.cn/govdata/gov/202403/19/513111/article.html). Official 2024 regulation; scope-specific current legal review is required. No universal refund deadline is inferred.

<a id="k03"></a>

## K03 人手不足 / Capacity shortage

- **Use for:** A small team cannot clear complaints and serve new customers simultaneously.
- **Decision questions:** How many hours of work are outstanding and what commitments must be met today?
- **Practical response:** Reduce new intake proportionately; assign one queue owner and a backup. Choose a realistic service tier before adding a communications campaign.
- **Organization:** The manager combines roles where needed and checks the most consequential commitments at shift handover.
- **Communication / media:** Publish usable opening or response times; avoid naming imaginary departments.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** More staff is a proposal until approved; redirecting all workers to publicity can worsen service.
- **Basis:** [CDC plain language](https://www.cdc.gov/health-literacy/php/develop-materials/plain-language.html). Audience-oriented wording, prioritized messages and usable instructions.

<a id="k04"></a>

## K04 供应商中断 / Supplier disruption

- **Use for:** An external dependency fails while customers hold your organization accountable.
- **Decision questions:** What can be substituted safely and contractually, who controls the dependency, and which promises are affected?
- **Practical response:** Map the affected service only, compare temporary alternatives and get customer consent where the substitution changes the deal.
- **Organization:** Procurement confirms supplier milestones; operations checks alternatives; the accountable owner retains customer responsibility.
- **Communication / media:** Explain effects and options without unsupported blame; keep the supplier dispute separate from customer relief.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** A second vendor is not an executable remedy until capacity, quality and funding are confirmed.
- **Basis:** [FTC merchandise orders](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/business-guide-ftcs-mail-internet-or-telephone-order-merchandise-rule). Shipment representations, delay consent and refunds for covered US merchandise. Do not generalize fixed deadlines to services or other markets.

<a id="k05"></a>

## K05 整改完成核对 / Completion checks

- **Use for:** Teams say a problem is fixed but users report continuing failure.
- **Decision questions:** What does fixed mean from the user's perspective, and has anyone tried the whole journey?
- **Practical response:** Test the affected journey, including accessibility and payment steps. Reopen in a limited scope with a clear rollback trigger.
- **Organization:** A service owner signs off on observed results; frontline staff log exceptions; leadership decides expansion.
- **Communication / media:** Report what was checked, when and what remains open; avoid permanent-prevention guarantees.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** A deployed patch or sent message alone does not establish recovery for all users.
- **Basis:** [CDC plain language](https://www.cdc.gov/health-literacy/php/develop-materials/plain-language.html). Audience-oriented wording, prioritized messages and usable instructions.
