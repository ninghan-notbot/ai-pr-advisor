# Products / 按需决策卡

Adapt these suggestions to the user’s facts, resources and authority. Use the working basis in [SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md#one-basis-for-action-and-copy).

<a id="k11"></a>

## K11 消费品召回 / Consumer recall

- **Use for:** A product defect may require retrieval, repair or refund.
- **Decision questions:** Which identifiers and batches are involved, what use creates risk and which regulator has jurisdiction?
- **Practical response:** Arrange proportionate protection, identify affected products clearly and design a remedy that customers can complete.
- **Organization:** Product safety leads scope; distribution blocks affected stock; service and finance implement the authorized remedy.
- **Communication / media:** State product identifiers, consumer action and assistance routes; verify recall completion, not just notice reach.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** Recall scope and legal duties require qualified review; an announcement is not evidence the hazard is removed.
- **Basis:** [CPSC recall guidance](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Recall-Guidance). Consumer product recall planning and effectiveness; not a jurisdiction-neutral legal checklist.

## K12 食品过敏原 / Food allergen concern

- **Use for:** Label or ingredient information may omit an allergen.
- **Decision questions:** Which recipe, lot, locations and consumers are affected, and is the product still being served?
- **Practical response:** Isolate implicated stock and have qualified food-safety staff determine scope and next actions. Make identification usable for customers.
- **Organization:** Food-safety lead coordinates kitchens, suppliers, retailers and regulator contact; a manager authorizes remedy costs.
- **Communication / media:** Prioritize actionable consumer notice and accessible contacts over an image campaign.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** Do not declare unaffected lots safe by inference or offer diagnosis; category-specific requirements must be checked.
- **Basis:** [FDA recall guidance](https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/industry-guidance-recalls). Index to food, drug and device recall guidance. Category-specific operational details below are original planning suggestions, not a verified summary of every linked regulation.

## K13 医疗服务延迟 / Healthcare access delay

- **Use for:** Appointments or results are delayed and patients lack guidance.
- **Decision questions:** Is there a clinical urgency, who can triage safely, and which non-digital route is available?
- **Practical response:** Have qualified clinical staff prioritize urgent needs while administrators restore access and contact affected patients privately.
- **Organization:** Clinical lead decides medical priority; scheduling and support arrange access; management approves added capacity.
- **Communication / media:** Explain service steps and contact routes without disclosing patient details or giving clinical assurances.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** A communications adviser cannot determine a patient's medical risk; apply local healthcare rules.
- **Basis:** [CDC CERC](https://www.cdc.gov/cerc/php/about/index.html). Public health emergency communication; adapted operational advice is the project's own synthesis.

## K14 批次质量投诉 / Quality complaints

- **Use for:** Several customers report defects but the extent is unclear.
- **Decision questions:** Are complaints linked by batch, channel or use, and is there an immediate hazard?
- **Practical response:** Preserve samples and complaint data, isolate the plausible scope and compare targeted inspection with wider action as evidence changes.
- **Organization:** Quality owner coordinates service, supply chain and an authorized decision maker; widen scope when the evidence warrants it.
- **Communication / media:** Separate customer reports from confirmed findings; explain the next check and remedy route.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** No complaints in another batch is not proof of zero risk; publicity cannot substitute for inspection.
- **Basis:** [CPSC recall guidance](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Recall-Guidance). Consumer product recall planning and effectiveness; not a jurisdiction-neutral legal checklist.

## K15 补救渠道堵塞 / Remedy bottleneck

- **Use for:** Customers cannot use an announced return, repair or replacement.
- **Decision questions:** What step fails, who lacks transport or digital access, and is replacement capacity real?
- **Practical response:** Remove unnecessary proof burdens, offer workable return routes and track successful receipt of the remedy.
- **Organization:** Service owns case completion; logistics confirms collection capacity; finance tracks costs and exceptions.
- **Communication / media:** Publish practical access instructions and realistic turnaround times, not just policy language.
- **Failure conditions / limits:** A replacement with the same unresolved defect is not a remedy; monitor repeat complaints.
- **Basis:** [CPSC recall guidance](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Recall-Guidance). Consumer product recall planning and effectiveness; not a jurisdiction-neutral legal checklist.
