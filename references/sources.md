# Public sources and limits

Checked 2026-11-17. Official pages were consulted for the stated scope. Original summaries and
operational applications are not source quotations or claims of endorsement. Linked third-party
works retain their rights; the project MIT license does not relicense them.

## cerc

[CDC CERC](https://www.cdc.gov/cerc/php/about/index.html)

Public health emergency communication; adapted operational advice is the project's own synthesis.

## breach

[FTC data breach response](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/data-breach-response-guide-business)

Response coordination, evidence preservation and scoped notification. US guidance; local legal duties need current review.

## ai

[NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework)

AI risk management; framework revision is in progress, so no certification or complete adoption is claimed.

## recall

[CPSC recall guidance](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Recall-Guidance)

Consumer product recall planning and effectiveness; not a jurisdiction-neutral legal checklist.

## fda

[FDA recall guidance](https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/industry-guidance-recalls)

Index to food, drug and device recall guidance. Category-specific operational details below are original planning suggestions, not a verified summary of every linked regulation.

## access

[DOJ effective communication](https://www.ada.gov/resources/effective-communication/)

US accessibility guidance. Check applicable law and individual needs; do not assume one format serves everyone.

## plain

[CDC plain language](https://www.cdc.gov/health-literacy/php/develop-materials/plain-language.html)

Audience-oriented wording, prioritized messages and usable instructions.

## participation

[EPA public participation](https://www.epa.gov/international-cooperation/public-participation-guide)

Meaningful public involvement in environmental decisions; adaptation to other organizations is an inference.

## workers

[OSHA worker participation](https://www.osha.gov/safety-management/worker-participation)

Worker and contractor participation without retaliation. Employment-law application remains jurisdiction-specific.

## ethics

[PRSA ethics](https://www.prsa.org/professional-development/prsa-resources/ethics)

Honesty, independence and fairness; professional ethical guidance is not law.

## endorsement

[FTC influencer disclosures](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers)

Material connections and clear disclosure; current local rules must be checked.

## green

[FTC Green Guides summary](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/environmental-claims-summary-green-guides)

Specific substantiated environmental claims, not broad unqualified benefits. The historical guidance is not a claim about every jurisdiction.

## orders

[FTC merchandise orders](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/business-guide-ftcs-mail-internet-or-telephone-order-merchandise-rule)

Shipment representations, delay consent and refunds for covered US merchandise. Do not generalize fixed deadlines to services or other markets.

## cnconsumer

[China consumer protection implementing regulation](https://app.www.gov.cn/govdata/gov/202403/19/513111/article.html)

Official 2024 regulation; scope-specific current legal review is required. No universal refund deadline is inferred.

## ap

[AP news tips](https://www.ap.org/contact-us/news-tips/)

Official public-interest tip route; not a promotional pitch promise.

## wire

[Business Wire pricing](https://www.businesswire.com/pricing)

Dated distribution starting price; neither a firm quote nor independent editorial coverage.
