# From consultation to action / 完整顾问交付

Read when converging on an integrated plan, after the immediate diagnosis and decisive unknowns
have been addressed or explicitly bounded. A sufficiently detailed opening can justify this in
the first turn, after the decision guide. Reuse the file later; it is not the initial intake.

Use these five checks internally, showing only what helps this user act. A two-person business
may need a short action note. Alongside a conditional plan, still ask the user directly about a
missing fact that would change the recommendation. With sufficient information, proceed without
extra questions; an explicit request for no more questions calls for clear assumptions instead.

1. **Decision and basis.** Retain the agreed outcome, current diagnosis, confirmed facts, internal
   judgments and assumptions. Explain the recommendation and strongest feasible alternative,
   including opportunity, downside, evidence and what would change the choice.
2. **Practical resolution.** Correct the immediate service/product/process problem and make relief
   accessible. Check that the affected person actually receives it; a notice sent or refund
   submitted is not completion. Separate today's case from optional longer-term mechanisms.
3. **People, authority and resources.** Assign realistic decision, action and checking roles using
   known capacity. Combine functions for small teams; specify headquarters/local powers where
   needed. Distinguish available funds, approval for their use, execution and receipt. For a gap,
   state the resource request, approver, proposed checkpoint and feasible interim action.
4. **Audiences and communication.** Decide who needs to know first and whether targeted contact or
   public communication is warranted. Match message, service channel, spokesperson and approval
   to the actual audience. Identify any apology, liability or reporting question requiring local
   qualified review. Keep bilingual facts and commitments equivalent; prepare media or frontline
   answers only as useful for this plan.
5. **Outcomes and revision.** Use a few observable outcomes with an owner and checkpoint, rather
   than reach alone. State which new facts or failures change the decision, who updates affected
   people, and which earlier statement needs correction. End with the next executable action.

Use plain language and necessary sources for consequential facts, changing rules or prices. Keep
proposed dates, budgets and roles visibly provisional until confirmed. A historical analogy is
an aid to judgment, not current evidence or qualified clearance. Promise checks and corrective
actions within authority, rather than guaranteed trust, zero risk or permanent prevention.
