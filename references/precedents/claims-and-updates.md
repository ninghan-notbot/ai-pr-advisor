# Claims and later developments

Researcher initial review, 2026-11-17; not independent audit or a statement of current law everywhere.
Decision rules are project inferences. Read dated source updates before using these analogies.

## P04 Fashion green claims: proof must match the claim's scope

**Observed:** In March 2024 ASOS, Boohoo and George at Asda signed CMA undertakings on clear,
accurate environmental claims, including specific material descriptions, collection criteria,
product filters, accreditation scope and verifiable target strategies, with compliance reporting.
This is one joint investigation here, not three newly counted crises.
[CMA investigation and undertakings](https://www.gov.uk/cma-cases/asos-boohoo-and-asda-greenwashing-investigation).

**Apply when:** Evidence for a component, period or pilot is being presented as whole-product or
company-wide proof. Ask what exactly was measured and what the audience will reasonably infer.
Technical/procurement owners verify scope; commerce checks filters and imagery; marketing corrects
all affected assets; an authorized reviewer checks consistency across markets.
**Failure boundary:** A footnote does not necessarily fix a misleading headline or visual. An
undertaking does not prove prior guilt or successful compliance for every item. Avoid substituting
another unsupported positive claim; retain a narrow truthful benefit when evidence supports it.

## P05 Simba: implementation and monitoring matter after a promise

**Observed:** Simba signed undertakings in July 2024 on discount and urgency claims, including
reference-price conditions and a compliance report. The CMA page records revised undertakings
accepted in September 2026 and published in October. The current terms must be checked before use.
[CMA Simba case](https://www.gov.uk/cma-cases/simba-sleep-limited-consumer-protection-case).

**Apply when:** A team proposes changing copy without changing promotion logic. Ask whether timers,
reference prices and actual sales histories agree. Commerce owns configuration; finance/data checks
underlying sales; the accountable owner schedules checks and escalation for recurrence.
**Failure boundary:** Do not import a sector-specific pricing test as universal law, or claim a signed
undertaking proves all later displays complied. A timer can be informative when it describes a real,
accurate deadline; automatic removal of all useful timing information is not the only option.

## P06 Emma: later findings can narrow the original narrative

**Observed:** The CMA case page records a May 2026 court-confirmed settlement on misleading urgency
and discount practices. Its July 2026 update reports admitted breaches but no further infringement
findings in the reference-pricing judgment, and temporary withdrawal of online mattress guidance
while the CMA considers next steps. These stages must not be collapsed into an unchanged 2024 allegation.
[CMA Emma case and later judgment update](https://www.gov.uk/cma-cases/emma-group-consumer-protection-case).

**Apply when:** A historical enforcement example is offered as today's legal or strategic answer.
Check the latest official procedural position and the exact issue decided; revise any recommendation
that relied on withdrawn guidance. Local qualified review owns legal interpretation; communications
must correct affected assertions across languages and channels.
**Failure boundary:** Withdrawal of guidance does not establish all challenged practices were lawful.
Different firms, evidence, commitments and dates prevent a simple 'cooperate = success' comparison.

## Compare before choosing

P04 tests claim-to-evidence fit, P05 tests implementation over time, and P06 tests whether later evidence
changes the rule being used. Use them to select questions and owners, not as a script, a guarantee or
a substitute for live investigation. Retain the strongest feasible alternative and its evidence conditions.
